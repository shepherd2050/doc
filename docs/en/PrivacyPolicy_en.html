1. Scope and Definitions

1.1 This policy applies to SeekEssen (hereinafter "this app" or "we"), a structured analysis tool for individual users, designed for continuously recording, analyzing, and consolidating users' analyses of various subjects.

1.2 This app is a single-device app: it has no server and does not upload your analysis content. If you enable iCloud backup, your data can be restored on other devices under the same Apple ID, but this app does not provide real-time multi-device synchronization.

1.3 This policy explains how we handle your information and the rights you have.

1.4 Definitions:

  • Personal Data: Information that can identify a specific natural person.
  • Analysis Content: All content you create in this app, including but not limited to objects, templates, and methods.
  • Logs: Operation logs and error logs recorded locally by this app, which do not contain your analysis content.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

2.1 We do not collect personally identifiable information. We have no server, do not upload your analysis content, and do not integrate any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

2.2 Analysis Content: The core content of this app, including but not limited to objects, templates, and methods, is stored entirely on your device. We cannot read it.

2.3 Crash Information: Crash information consists of iOS system information. When it is needed to diagnose issues, you must share it manually with the developer before we can receive it. We do not upload or collect it automatically.

2.4 Error Logs: To facilitate troubleshooting, this app records operation logs and error logs locally. The logs do not contain your analysis content, are limited to 10 MB in size, and will not grow indefinitely. The logs are stored only on your device, and you must share them manually within this app with the developer before we can receive them. We do not upload or collect them automatically.

2.5 Except for the logs and crash information that you share manually as described above, this app does not transmit data to any server.

3. Data Storage and iCloud

3.1 Your analysis content is stored on your device by default.

3.2 If you enable iCloud backup, your data will be included in iCloud backups and carried by Apple's iCloud service. Such backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, and we cannot access the backup content in iCloud.

3.3 Through iCloud backup, you can restore your data on other devices under the same Apple ID; this app does not provide real-time multi-device synchronization.

3.4 If you have not enabled iCloud backup and have not made a manual backup, your data will be lost after uninstalling the app. If you have made a manual backup, you can restore your data using that backup after reinstalling the app.

3.5 This app does not carry out any cross-border data transfers.

4. How We Protect Your Data

4.1 Encryption is optional: This app offers an encryption feature, which you decide whether to enable. Once enabled, your data is stored encrypted on your device, and the key is derived from your master password and never leaves your device.

4.2 We do not hold your key: We (the developer) cannot know whether you have enabled encryption, do not hold your key, and cannot decrypt your data.

4.3 Keys and Recovery: If you enable encryption, the system generates a recovery key when you set your password, which you must write down by hand and keep offline. We do not store the recovery key. If you lose the recovery key and forget your master password, your data will be permanently unrecoverable. This is the security trade-off of the encryption mechanism, and we cannot bypass it.

4.4 No Synchronization Feature: This app does not provide real-time synchronization. Your data is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to any server (except for iCloud backups, see Section 3).

4.5 Third-Party Responsibility Boundary: iCloud backup is provided by Apple and governed by its privacy policy; apart from that, this app does not rely on any third-party data collection service.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, and delete your data;
  • Export or back up your data.

Because your data is stored on your device, the above operations are all performed on your device, and we cannot perform them on your behalf.

6. Data Retention

6.1 Your data remains on your device until you delete it yourself.

6.2 If you have not enabled iCloud backup and have not made a manual backup, your data will be lost after uninstalling the app.

6.3 If you have made a manual backup, you can restore your data using that backup after reinstalling the app.

7.2 If the jurisdiction in which you reside sets a higher age threshold for children (e.g., 14 or 16), the local law shall prevail.
